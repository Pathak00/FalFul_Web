Our Vision

How It Started

The problem we set out to solve

Walk into any supermarket and you'll find fruit that's been sitting in cold storage for days — sometimes weeks. The taste, texture, and nutrition are long gone by the time it reaches your table.

The farmers growing the best fruit in Pokhara and Kathmandu Valley had no direct channel to health-conscious consumers who would pay fairly for freshness. The two groups were separated by layers of distributors, each one adding cost and subtracting quality.

FalFul closes that gap. We partner directly with verified farms, harvest to your order, and deliver within 48 hours — every single time.
